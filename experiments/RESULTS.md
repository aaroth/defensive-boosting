# Online boosting experiment output

This directory is generated by `python3 -m experiments.run`.

The compared algorithms are the Defensive Booster, single-learner unboosted regression and classification controls, online gradient boosting for squared loss, Beygelzimer-Kale-Luo Online BBM and AdaBoost.OL.W, Chen-Lin-Lu online SmoothBoost with the OCP combiner, and a Brier-loss aggregator over the four ensembles. The aggregator combines their forecasts before observing the current label and updates its weights afterward. Labels are encoded in {-1,1}, and prediction scores in [-1,1] are converted to probabilities before scoring. The unboosted classifier and BBM make hard binary predictions, so their Brier score is the Brier score of the induced 0/1 probability forecast. AdaBoost.OL's Hedge probability is scored directly; its randomized error is the expected error of its randomized classifier. The BBM-vote row is a separate diagnostic that scores its normalized raw vote as a probability.

No run chooses parameters by optimizing observed performance on an individual stream. Linear-loss methods use the `second_order` oracle variant. The Defensive Booster uses the parameter-free scalar adaptive-OGD updates specified in the paper. OGB, BBM, AdaBoost.OL, and OSBoost use the requested number of weak learners or learner-sweep values; OGB uses stage step `(log N)/N` for `N > 1` and step `1` for `N = 1`. BBM and OSBoost use a guaranteed synthetic advantage when one is known: `gamma=0.08` on binary aggregation and `gamma=0.1` otherwise. AdaBoost.OL is parameter-free apart from its ensemble size and shared base learner.
